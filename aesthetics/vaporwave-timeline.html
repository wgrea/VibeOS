⏳ Vaporwave & Subculture Chronology

1. Pre-Vaporwave Foundations (2009–2010)

Hypnagogic Pop (David Keenan / cassette-degraded 80s pop) and Eccojams (Daniel Lopatin / micro-looping sampling).

2. The Core Explosion (2011–2012)

Seapunk (@lilinternet), Vaporwave (James Ferraro / Tumblr), Classic Vapor (Macintosh Plus), and Vaportrap (Blank Banshee).

3. First Wave Expansion (2012–2014)

Mallsoft, Future Funk (Saint Pepsi), Utopian Virtual, Weatherwave (Eco Virtual), and VHS Pop / Late Night Lo-Fi.

4. Mid-Era Shifts & Deconstruction (2014–2016)

Slushwave (t e l e p a t h), Vapormeme, Vapornoise, Hardvapour (wosX), and Signal Waves.

5. Critical Codification & Microgenres (2015–2019)

Faux-Utopian / Future Visions and Segahaze (16-bit/32-bit game menu nostalgia).

6. Modern Wave (2020–Present)

Barberbeats (Macroblank), Deathdream/Spookwave, Vaporjungle, Metrosong/Mallwave Revival, and Meta-Vapor original compositions.
