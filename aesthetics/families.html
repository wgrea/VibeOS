🏛️ Aesthetic Families & Structural Movements

💻 Pre-Computer / Old UI

Mechanical typewriters, analog dials, CRT phosphor burn, bulky beige plastic casings, and raw tactile machinery.

🌐 Frutiger Aero

Glossy textures, skeuomorphism, water droplets, glass UI, aurora gradients, and optimistic mid-2000s web tech.

🔮 Vaporwave

Post-industrial consumerism, slowed R&B, shopping mall Muzak, CRT static, and nostalgic irony.

🧸 Kid-Core

Primary colors, 1990s/2000s children's television, sticker sheets, plush textures, and playrooms.

💥 Comic Book

Ben-Day dots, bold ink outlines, halftone gradients, high-contrast action frames, and print textures.

🎸 Grungecore

Basement concrete floor weight, spinning vinyl records, raw analog distortion, and tactile grit.

🌿 Anti-Hustle Culture

Radical deceleration, spatial shields, domestic comfort, and complete detachment from productivity demands.
