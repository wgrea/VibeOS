🛡️ Anti-Hustle Taxonomy (Comfort Shields)

🏡 Cottagecore

The deep warmth of shelter while rain hits a tin roof outside, completely removed from time.

🪵 Cabincore / Goblincore (Isolationist)

Total detachment; comfort in damp soil, heavy knit sweaters, and dense forest silence.

🛏️ Slowcore / Bedcore (Passive Refusal)

An afternoon under a heavy duvet watching dust motes drift in sunlight while the world rushes past.

☕ Lo-Fi Study / Chillhop (Rhythmic Shield)

Predictable, repetitive rhythm to block productivity demands over a single low-stakes task.
