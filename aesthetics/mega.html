🧠 Core Mega-Aesthetics (Sensory Profiles)

Anemoia-Core (Yesterday's Tomorrow)

Aching nostalgia for a time you never lived. Vinyl scratches, typewriter thuds, golden-hour oxidized hues (mustard, avocado, sepia), and melancholic ghost-nesting comfort.

Vitreous Bleed (Midwest Monologue)

Emo-inspired regional tension. Concrete basement acoustics, sharp clean-to-distortion musical friction, and hyper-localized suburban melodrama.

Saccharine Shield (Neon Pastel Regress)

Weaponized softness and defensive regression. Chibi proportions, high-friction pastel matrices (strawberry milk, lavender), Yume, Yami, and Guro Kawaii.

Muted Luminance (Powdered Silicon)

Suspended weightlessness, diffused light saturation, matte textures (heavy paper, drywall), and achromatic calm.
