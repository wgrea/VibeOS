⚡ Punk Genres & Counterculture Spectrum

⚡ Cyberpunk

High tech, low life. Neon rain, wire clutter, corporate dominance.

⚙️ Steampunk

Victorian industrialism, brass gears, steam-powered machinery.

🦇 Post-Punk / Goth

Angular guitars, cold wave synths, monochrome, existential angst.

🔥 Hardcore / Crust Punk

Raw velocity, distorted bass walls, DIY zines, basement shows.

☀️ Solarpunk

Ecological optimism, renewable architecture, community gardens.

📼 Cassettepunk

Analog micro-computing, magnetic tape, beige plastic casings.
