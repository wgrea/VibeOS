🌐 Frutiger Aero & UI Design Evolution

1. Pre-Aero Era: Vector Art & Web 2.0 (2000–2005)

Vectorgarden, Frutiger Metro (iPod commercials, Zune), and Musica Metro (concentric soundwaves).

2. The Core Frutiger Aero Peak (2005–2011)

Frutiger Aero (Windows Vista/7, Wii), Frutiger Aqua, Frutiger Eco, Technozen, and Helvetica Aqua Aero.

3. Mid-to-Late 2000s Hardware & Sub-Aesthetics (2007–2011)

Electro Pop 08, Bright Tertiaries, Dark Aero, Four Colors, and High Clean Core (MacBook unibodies).

4. Late Aero Transitions & Cold Futurism (2009–2013)

Futurism Aero, Frutiger Aurora, Metal Heart (chrome/cooling pipes), and Cyber Glacier.
