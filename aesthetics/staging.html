🏛️ Top-Level Mega-Aesthetics to Add

Major standalone aesthetic wings alongside y2k/ to round out the VibeOS directory:

  • Academia (academia/): Dark & Light Academia—mahogany libraries, floating parchment pages, brass astrolabes, candlelight, classical busts, and rain-streaked gothic arch windows.
  • Sci-Fi & Futurism (sci-fi/): Dedicated future wing spanning Cyberpunk (neon rain & techwear), Cassette Futurism (1970s/80s amber CRT switches & brushed aluminum), and Solarpunk (glass bio-domes & brass turbines).
  • Webcore & Liminal Surrealism (webcore/): Early 1995–2008 Old Web / Geocities (3D "Under Construction" signs, spinning MIDI icons) paired with Dreamcore / Weirdcore (endless checkered plains & floating doors).
  • Glam & Runway Maximalism (glam/): Chrome catwalk stages, disco mirror balls, rhinestone sparkle shaders, and velvet ropes—built as a natural showcase for the Outfit Lab and DJ Lab.
  • Indie Sleaze (indie-sleaze/): 2006–2012 underground club & Tumblr/MySpace nostalgia—harsh disposable-camera flash lighting, tangled wired headphones, Polaroid collages, and grainy VHS overlays.

✍️ Anti-AI & Human-Made Analog Aesthetics

Counter-culture movements celebrating tangible human imperfection, physical media, and handcrafted artifacts in response to synthetic AI slop:

  • Zine & Risograph Craft (sensory-layers/ or webcore/): Hand-cut paper collages, halftone risograph ink misregistration, masking tape strips, spiral notebook margins, and visible pencil/brush strokes.
  • Neo-Brutalist / Anti-Design Web (ui-history/): Raw, honest digital architecture—exposed structural grids, default system monospaced fonts, hard 1px borders, and zero glossy drop-shadows.
  • Analog & Dumbphone Minimalism (comfort/): Deliberate offline calm—matte E-Ink monochrome displays, tactile flip phones, cassette walkmans, mechanical typewriters, and fountain pens.
  • Raw Lo-Fi / Unretouched Film (indie-sleaze/): Authentic 35mm film grain, light leaks, red-eye flash snapshots, and camcorder timecodes that reject hyper-smooth synthetic renders.

🌐 Emerging & Modern Aesthetics to Add

  • Corecore (dark/ or vaporwave/): Fragmented multi-screen CRT/phone feed collage, overlapping lo-fi clips, nostalgic melancholy, and sensory-overload editing aesthetic.
  • Hopecore (comfort/): Uplifting counterpart to Corecore—warm golden sunbeams, glass prism light flares, blooming wildflowers, and drifting dandelion seeds.
  • Older Brother Core (gaming/): Mid-2000s teenage bedroom nostalgia—stacked CRT TV + PS2/Xbox console, leaned electric guitar, checkered rug, and glowing green lava lamp.
  • Y3K / Hyper-Chrome Futurism (frutiger-aero/): Gen-Z/Alpha evolution of Y2K—liquid mercury chrome metaballs, iridescent holographic glass visors, and spiky silver star emblems.
  • Surveillance / CCTV Core (dark/): Grainy night-vision green security camera feeds, sweeping spotlight cone, blinking red REC tally light, and timestamp overlays.
  • Heisei Retro (kidcore/): Late-1990s/2000s Japanese consumer pop nostalgia—translucent atomic-purple/pink MiniDisc players, pastel flip phones, and beaded charms.

💿 Y2K Sub-Aesthetics to Add (1998–2006)

Sub-taxonomy tracking the shift from millennium cyber-optimism to mid-2000s pop maximalism:

  • Cyber-Y2K (Silver / Ice Blue): Liquid chrome, translucent Bondi-blue CRT hardware, metallic outerwear, and sleek sci-fi optimism (1998–2001).
  • Dark Y2K / Cyber-Club: Nighttime rave and Matrix-era edge—glossy black PVC, glowing neon cyan/magenta accents, and tinted wrap-around visor shades.
  • McBling (2003–2006): Post-Y2K transition into hot pink, rhinestones, velour tracksuits, flip phones, and maximalist pop glamour.
