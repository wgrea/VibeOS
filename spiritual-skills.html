01. The Interaction Kernel Concept

Rather than treating competencies as static inventory items, skills function as an Interaction Kernel—managing the low-level orchestration of human attention, sensory input, and systemic state.

  • Monolithic vs. Microkernel Models: Moving from tightly coupled, rigid workflows (high friction on state change) to lean, decoupled microkernels where UI modules and ambient layers operate as isolated services.
  • Global State Orchestration ($NAV & $VIBE): The $NAV Register coordinates spatial data-paths and trajectories, while the $VIBE Register broadcasts atmospheric tone-tokens across all active DOM nodes simultaneously.

04. The Detachment / Attachment Cycle & Comparative Metaphysics

Managing internal system state requires navigating the cycle of attachment (Upadana) and release (Nirodha). Different spiritual architectures introduce unique operational layers:

  • Buddhist Framework: Maps suffering to craving (Tanha) and rigid identity-locking (Upadana). Liberation (Upekkha) is achieved through direct insight into impermanence (Anicca).
  • Taoism (Wu-Wei): Aligns detachment with natural flow rather than forceful suppression, treating disruptions as transient weather.
  • Stoicism (Ataraxia): Establishes a logical firewall separating internal agency from external outcomes, neutralizing misdirected attachment.
  • Advaita Vedanta (Viveka & Vairagya): Discriminating between the transient ego-interface and the unchanging observer consciousness (Atman).
  • Sufism (Fana & Baqā): Intentional dissolution of the ego-self into the infinite, resolving attachment through transcendent devotion.
