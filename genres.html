II. Audio & Atmosphere Integration

Sound Layers

  • Warm Mechanical: Gears, relay switches, tape head hiss, motor hum.
  • Quiet Room Hum: 60Hz/50Hz mains electricity vibration, distant radiator pipes.
  • Night Drone: Deep synth-free sub-bass rumble, highway static, wind against glass.
  • Airplane Cabin: Pink noise saturation, pressurized air whistle, cabin vibration.
  • Ambient Nothingness: Near-absolute silence, room resonance, floorboard creaks.

Narration Layers

  • Deadpan Micro-Horror: Unemotional delivery of disturbing environmental logs.
  • Monotone Explanatory: Cold, industrial 1970s training film voiceovers.
  • Soft Domestic: Warm, intimate mid-range cassette letter recordings.
  • Transit: Garbled tannoy announcements, conductor squawks, station echo.
  • Folklore / Myth: Weathered, rhythmic oral tradition storytelling cadence.
